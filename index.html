How It Works

Step 01

Data Collection

Gather temperature, rainfall, water price, and carbon data from sources such as NOAA, EIA, and water stress datasets.

Step 02

Normalization

Apply min-max scaling so variables are converted to a comparable 0-1 range.

Step 03

Index Calculation

Compute Water Risk, Climate Load, Carbon Impact, and Energy Cost pillars (each 0–1), then blend into Total Impact.

Step 04

Weighting

Apply adjustable weights to reflect planning priorities like sustainability, carbon reduction, or cost control.

Step 05

Visualization

Display outputs in charts, rankings, and an overall Total Impact Score for clear comparison.

Input Variables

Avg Annual Temperature

Long-term climate baseline for each city.

Avg Summer Temperature

Primary indicator for cooling demand.

Cooling Degree Days

Cumulative measure of cooling needs over time.

Annual Precipitation

Proxy indicator for regional water availability.

Water Price

Operating cost pressure linked to water use.

Water Stress Index

Relative scarcity and regional water constraints.

Drought Risk

Likelihood of drought-related disruptions.

Grid Carbon Intensity

Emissions impact of consumed electricity.

Electricity Price

Key driver of total operating cost.

Output Indices

Water Risk Score

Blend of stress, drought, price, dryness, and monthly rainfall variability (0–1, higher = worse).

Climate Load Index

Summer heat, cooling degree days, annual temperature, and heating demand (0–1).

Carbon Impact Score

Mean of normalized grid intensity and merged state CO₂ / MWh signals when available (0–1).

Energy Cost Score

Electricity price proxy plus optional industrial total from the grid workbook (0–1).

Total Impact Score

Weighted combination of the four pillars on a 0–10 scale; lower is better.

About

Why Water Matters for Data Centers

  • Data centers are growing water consumers.
  • Siting decisions often ignore water risk.
  • This tool makes trade-offs visible.

The Problem

Teams must balance cost, carbon, and water risk while planning resilient, sustainable infrastructure.

Our Solution

An interactive dashboard with weighted scoring to compare locations under different strategy scenarios.

The Outcome

A practical decision-making tool for planners and engineers evaluating data center site options.
