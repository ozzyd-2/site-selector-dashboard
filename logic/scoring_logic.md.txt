Site Selector Dashboard – Scoring Logic
Purpose
The Site Selector Dashboard evaluates potential locations for building a new data center facility by converting environmental and infrastructure data into comparable risk indicators.
The algorithm transforms raw city-level data into three intermediate metrics:
• Water Risk Score
• Cooling Cost Index
• Carbon Cost
These metrics are combined into a single Total Impact Score that allows cities to be compared within the dashboard.
Lower Total Impact Scores represent more favorable locations for a facility.
The scores are intended for relative comparison between cities rather than exact cost prediction.

3.2 Equations
1. Water Risk Score
Water risk reflects the likelihood that water scarcity, drought, or high water costs could impact facility operations.

Water_Risk_Score = (Water_Stress_Index × 2)

(Drought_Risk × 1.5)

(Water_Price × 1)

Where:
• Water_Stress_Index represents long-term water scarcity conditions
• Drought_Risk represents the probability of drought conditions
• Water_Price represents the cost of water per 1000 gallons
Water stress receives the highest weighting because data centers rely heavily on water for cooling systems.

2. Cooling Cost Index
Cooling demand increases in warmer climates. The Cooling Cost Index estimates cooling-related energy demand.

Cooling_Cost_Index = Avg_Summer_Temp × Electricity_Price

Where:
• Avg_Summer_Temp represents the average summer temperature
• Electricity_Price represents the cost of electricity
This index estimates how expensive it may be to operate cooling systems in hotter climates.

3. Carbon Cost
Carbon cost represents the environmental impact associated with electricity consumption.
Carbon_Cost = Grid_Carbon_Intensity × Electricity_Price
Where:
• Grid_Carbon_Intensity represents kilograms of CO₂ emitted per MWh of electricity generation.
  Higher carbon intensity means the same electricity usage produces greater emissions.

4. Total Impact Score
The Total Impact Score combines water risk, cooling cost, and carbon cost into a single comparative metric.
Total_Impact_Score = (Water_Risk_Score × 2)

(Cooling_Cost_Index × 1)

(Carbon_Cost × 1.5)

Weighting rationale:

• Water Risk receives the highest weighting (×2) because water availability is critical for cooling reliability.
• Carbon Cost receives a moderate weighting (×1.5) to account for sustainability impacts and potential carbon regulations.
• Cooling Cost receives a lower weighting (×1) because it primarily affects operational energy costs rather than system feasibility.

3.3 Scenario Logic

The dashboard can explore alternative decision priorities by adjusting the weighting of each indicator.
Base Case – Balanced Sustainability

Water Risk Weight = 2
Cooling Cost Weight = 1
Carbon Cost Weight = 1.5

Carbon Priority Scenario

Water Risk Weight = 1.5
Cooling Cost Weight = 1
Carbon Cost Weight = 3

This scenario represents organizations prioritizing low-carbon electricity grids.

Water Conservation Scenario

Water Risk Weight = 3
Cooling Cost Weight = 1
Carbon Cost Weight = 1

This scenario emphasizes long-term water security.

These alternative weightings allow the dashboard to demonstrate how site rankings change depending on organizational priorities.

3.4 Assumptions and Trade-offs
Several simplifying assumptions were made for this prototype decision-support tool.

State-level electricity data: Grid carbon intensity is calculated at the state level and used as a proxy for city-level electricity generation mixes.

Effective water price: Water price represents an effective volumetric price based on standardized household usage rather than the exact marginal rate from utilities.

Climate indicators: Average summer temperature is used as a simplified proxy for cooling demand rather than modeling detailed building energy simulations.

Relative comparison: All calculated scores are intended for relative comparison between cities, not precise financial cost prediction.

Data Flow for the Dashboard
The scoring logic supports the dashboard through the following workflow:

City Data (CSV File)
       ↓
Python Scoring Algorithm
       ↓
Calculated Risk & Cost Scores
       ↓
Dashboard Visualization
       ↓
User Comparison of Cities

When a user selects a city from the dropdown menu, the dashboard displays:
• Monthly rainfall bar chart
• Water Risk Score
• Cooling Cost Index
• Carbon Cost
• Total Impact Score
This allows decision makers to quickly compare environmental and operational trade-offs between potential locations.
